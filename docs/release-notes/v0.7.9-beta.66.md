### Beta highlights

This is an Android Mobile and Android TV hotfix for Free P2P playback problems reported in beta.65.

- **Slow-starting streams are no longer abandoned.** In beta.65 an automatic Play could close the player after its 30-second startup notice, or after an early player error, and move on to another source. The player now stays open and keeps trying, as in beta.64. Automatic fallback still happens only when a torrent fails before the player opens.
- **Automatic choice uses measured live speed again.** Normal Play and Quick Play choose by measured first byte, throughput, live peers and compatibility. In beta.65 the Source Priority could make them prefer the largest, highest-quality release. Your Source Priority still orders the list in My Priority.
- **Android TV: Play opens the source list right away.** Play on a title or episode shows the sources as soon as the providers answer, as in beta.64. The live check runs in the background and every row can be played at once.
- **Fewer repeated checks.** Reopening the sources or pressing Play again on the same title reuses live-check results from the last three minutes. Re-check still starts over.
- **Shorter playback notices.** The "Could not play" and "did not start" messages close by themselves after a few seconds, can be closed on mobile, and replace each other instead of stacking. The playback report can still be copied from the source list on mobile, or from the Live report chip on TV.
- Cloud/debrid playback (TorBox, Real-Debrid, Premiumize, PikPak) and direct links are unchanged.

**Still under validation:** these fixes are covered by automated tests only. Free P2P playback on real phones and TVs, including the titles reported in beta.65, still needs testing on devices. Whether a torrent plays also depends on its swarm at that moment. Please share the playback report if a source does not start.
