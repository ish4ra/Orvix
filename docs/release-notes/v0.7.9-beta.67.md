### Beta highlights

Urgent Android Mobile and Android TV Free P2P recovery hotfix after playback and browsing regressions in beta.65–66.

- **Manual Free P2P playback restored.** Play on a title, episode or Continue Watching now opens the source list. Select the torrent you want to try; Orvix no longer auto-picks or automatically hops between torrents on Android.
- **No blocking live-probe round.** Browsing Android Free P2P sources does not launch the separate health-check torrents, show the 1/6–6/6 probe overlay, or require a probe verdict before a manual selection.
- **Simpler source list.** Removed Free P2P Recommended / My Priority / Smooth modes, health bands, Re-check and live-report controls. Provider, file size, reported seeders, compatibility, sorting and pins remain available.
- **Slow starts are not immediately treated as failed playback.** For a local Android torrent, the 30-second startup notice now says the player is still connecting, rather than displaying a full-screen failure card. Real player errors and Back remain functional.
- **Less intrusive failure notices.** Error notifications are brief and dismissible.
- Cloud/debrid routes, subtitles and the underlying torrent engine are not changed by this recovery patch.

**Validation notice:** automated tests and CI pass, but playback of specific torrents on a real Android phone or TV has not yet been confirmed. Torrent availability and startup times vary with the swarm. Please report actual failures with privacy-safe diagnostics.
