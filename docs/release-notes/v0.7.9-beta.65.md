### Beta highlights

This is an Android Mobile and Android TV test release focused on Free P2P playback reliability.

- **One-click Play on Android TV.** With no cloud/debrid account connected, Play on a title or episode now starts the best verified Free P2P source directly instead of opening the source browser. Manual choice stays one press away: use the new **Sources** button next to Play, or hold OK on an episode.
- **One-click Play on Android Mobile.** Play runs a bounded live check, starts the best verified source and, if it does not start, moves to the next verified source.
- **Verified-live sources only.** Automatic playback starts only direct links or torrents confirmed live by a recent check. Unchecked, dead or failed torrents are never auto-started, whatever their reported seeders.
- **Bounded automatic fallback.** At most three attempts per press, no new fallback after two minutes, and no source switching once playback has started or after Back. If nothing starts, Orvix says why and opens the source list.
- **Hybrid source ranking** combines measured live health with your Source Priority.
- **Session cleanup.** Torrent sessions survive the hand-off from live check to playback, and failed or abandoned attempts no longer leave torrents connecting in the background. Fixes the local torrent not being released after leaving ExoPlayer.
- **Playback diagnostics.** The live-check report now records each attempt and why it ended, with torrents and URLs redacted. Copy it from the source list on mobile or the Live report chip on TV.
- **Mobile Search** keyboard and focus fixes.
- Cloud/debrid playback (TorBox, Real-Debrid, Premiumize, PikPak) is unchanged.

**Still under validation:** Free P2P reliability on real phones and TVs, including specific torrents reported as failing to start, is still being tested. Please share the playback report if a source does not start.
