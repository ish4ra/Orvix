### Beta highlights

This is a private Android Mobile and Android TV test build focused on the ExoPlayer (Android Media3) player. Free P2P, cloud and debrid sources, MPV, AI Sinhala, Windows, macOS and iPhone/iPad are unchanged.

- **Android TV plays in ExoPlayer by default.** Auto on Android TV now opens ExoPlayer, which decodes in hardware and avoids the lag MPV shows on some TVs. A saved MPV choice is kept, and MPV stays available in Settings and from the player.
- **Android phones keep MPV as the default for now.** ExoPlayer on phones is a manual choice in Settings while it is tested on real devices. Phones and TVs now keep separate player choices.
- **No more false "could not initialize within 35 seconds".** ExoPlayer no longer gives up on a slow cloud or debrid source after a fixed time. The loading screen shows what is happening (connecting, loading video, starting playback, how much has arrived), and only a real error from the player ends the attempt.
- **Clear errors, and you choose what happens next.** A failure now says whether the problem is the connection, the source (for example an expired link), the file format or a codec this device cannot decode. The error screen offers Try MPV, Retry and Details; the player is never switched behind your back.
- **Subtitle styling that really changes the subtitles.** Size, font, weight, text color and opacity, outline or shadow style, edge color, outline thickness, shadow intensity, line background color and opacity, a background window, position, alignment and line spacing, with a live preview, saved between sessions and resettable. Timing can be moved earlier or later and reset, and stays in place when you seek. Turning subtitles off, and the subtitle and audio languages you pick, are remembered.
- **Fit, Fill and Zoom without stretching.** Fill now crops to fill the screen instead of stretching the picture, Zoom enlarges by an adjustable level, and the chosen mode is remembered.
- **Better remote control on TV.** Focus returns to the button that opened a menu, menus never lose focus to the controls behind them, Left/Right change a setting and Up/Down move between settings, holding Left/Right scrubs the timeline and seeks once, media Play/Pause/Rewind/Fast-forward keys always work, and the Menu key opens player settings. The skip step (5–60 s) is adjustable.
- **The screen stays on while ExoPlayer plays**, so a TV screensaver or a phone's screen timeout no longer interrupts a film.
- **Phones play in landscape fullscreen** and return to the app's normal rotation and system bars afterwards; tablets keep their orientation.
- **Playback info and a copyable report** (Settings → Playback info) show the codec, decoder, network progress and any error code, without links, hashes or account details, to help diagnose a source that will not play.

**Still under validation:** every change is covered by automated tests only. ExoPlayer playback, subtitles, audio tracks, picture modes, orientation and remote control on real phones and TVs still need testing on devices before this build is offered to anyone else.
