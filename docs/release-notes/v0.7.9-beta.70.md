### Beta highlights

This is an Android Mobile and Android TV release focused on Free P2P source browsing and playback reliability. The title details, source list, episode cards and TV layout stay as they were in beta.68 and beta.69.

- **The source list opens at once, everywhere.** Play, Find Sources, episodes, pinned sources and Continue Watching all open the source list as soon as the providers answer. No entry point waits for a "Checking live P2P sources… 1/6" round any more, and opening a title starts no torrent.
- **Any source can be played when you choose it.** Quick Play, a tapped row and OK on TV start the source straight away, checked or not. No background check competes with the torrent you chose; on TV, Re-check live only runs when you press it and stops once playback starts.
- **Slow torrents are no longer treated as failed.** A torrent may need minutes before its first frame. Orvix keeps waiting with a calm "Still connecting to peers…" note, keeps the same source and player, and only reports a failure when the player or the torrent engine really cannot continue. Magnet metadata also gets as long as it needs, and Back always works.
- **No penalties for slow starts.** A slow start is never recorded as a failed source, and earlier "taking longer than expected" records no longer push sources down the list.
- **Stable source order.** Identical provider results are always listed in the same order, so differences between devices now come only from different provider answers, settings, history or pins.
- **ExoPlayer is the default Android player.** Installs without a saved choice (and Auto) now use ExoPlayer on phones and TVs. It shows embedded subtitles, OpenSubtitles and subtitle files, with track, size and timing controls, plus audio tracks, speed, Fit / Fill / Zoom and next episode, in Orvix colors and with full remote control on TV. AI Sinhala still uses MPV automatically, and MPV stays available in Settings. A saved MPV or ExoPlayer choice is kept.
- **No more surprise keyboard.** On phones, opening Search or returning to it no longer raises the keyboard by itself; it opens when you tap the search field.
- Cloud/debrid playback (TorBox, Real-Debrid, Premiumize, PikPak) and direct links are unchanged.

**Still under validation:** these changes are covered by automated tests only. Free P2P playback and the new ExoPlayer screen on real phones and TVs still need testing on devices, and whether a torrent plays also depends on its swarm at that moment.
