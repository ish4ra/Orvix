### Beta highlights

This is a private test build that combines the new Android ExoPlayer player with the redesigned Android TV and phone screens and the private updater diagnostics. It includes every change in v0.7.10-beta.1, plus the following. MPV, Free P2P, cloud and debrid sources, subtitles in MPV and AI Sinhala are unchanged.

- **Android TV plays in ExoPlayer by default; phones keep MPV.** The ExoPlayer changes from v0.7.10-beta.1 are all included: startup without a fixed timeout, clear errors with Try MPV / Retry / Details, styled subtitles, Fit / Fill / Zoom without stretching, better remote control, the screen kept awake, and landscape fullscreen on phones.
- **Check for updates in Settings.** A new *App updates* section at the top of Settings checks for updates on demand and shows one clear result: up to date, an update available, or GitHub unreachable. *Install update* uses the existing download, checksum and install path.
- **Update prompt on Android TV works with the remote.** Press Up at the top of a screen to reach the banner (Update, What's new, Later). Down or Back returns to where you were.
- **Redesigned Android TV menu.** Focus and selection now look different: focus is a solid lime pill, and the selected screen has a lime marker and icon. Items are centred and sized for TV, and Settings, Account, Support and About sit below a divider.
- **Full-screen Android TV launcher banner**, with no dark bars on the TV home screen.
- **Continue Watching cards on Android TV** match the Windows and phone cards. They show the episode still, the time remaining or "Up next", the episode or year line, and a progress bar.
- **Phone QR scanner for TV sign-in.** The scanner explains why it needs the camera before asking for it. Once Android stops asking, it can open the app's settings and checks the permission again when you return. It also handles phones without a camera, and *Enter TV code* lets you type the code instead. Error messages no longer overlap the camera frame.
- **Clearer icons.** Decorative sparkle-style icons were replaced with icons that show what each control does.

**Still under validation:** every change is covered by automated tests only. ExoPlayer playback and the new TV, phone and updater screens still need testing on real devices before this build is offered to anyone else.
